# Character and attraction policy (LONG-FORM ONLY; approved by Joe 2026-11-03)
The Shorts rules (memory/short-form/learnings.md) are separate and unchanged. The mascot's look never changes: plain white round head, dot eyes, simple eyebrows, thin black stick limbs, bright yellow scarf always, scarf the only yellow.

## Tiers (set per concept; Joe is told about every tier 2)
- Tier 0 neutral: no romantic or suggestive element.
- Tier 1 romantic tension: couples, flirting, jealousy, love triangles, scandal faces, secrets, forbidden romance, power dynamics.
- Tier 2 cheeky/innuendo: adult characters, fully clothed, adult-oriented humor and suggestive situations when relevant to the story, no emphasis on body parts.
- Nothing above tier 2.

## Always
- Every character in a romantic or suggestive situation is clearly an adult (state age/role in the brief). Never minors or ambiguous ages.
- Non-explicit, mainstream-YouTube suitable: no explicit acts, no exposed intimate anatomy, no pornography, nothing degrading, exploitative or non-consensual.
- Thumbnails show something that actually appears in the video; never misrepresent it.
- Attractive female and male adult characters only when they have a meaningful role in the story. Attraction is one tool, not the channel's identity; do not sexualize every thumbnail.
- Attractiveness comes from styling (hair, outfit, posture, expression, color), not anatomy emphasis. Stickman style stays.
- Real people: no invented intimate details or dialogue; allegations are labelled; no private sex-life speculation as fact.
- Every pillar-6 or tier >= 1 concept goes through the policy check in packaging: YouTube advertiser-friendly/sexual-content guidance (see policy-notes.md) and age-restriction risk. Flag risk to Joe; never claim a concept is "monetization safe" without that check.
- Do not assume an attractive character raises CTR: test it (thumbnail-experiments.md).
