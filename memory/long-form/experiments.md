| # | Hypothesis | How we test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

| L2 | Character-driven stories retain better than fact-driven ones | Compare avg view duration | open |
| L3 | Short titles beat descriptive titles on CTR | Title patterns in video log | open |
| L4 | Expressive-face thumbnails raise CTR | Thumbnail style per video | open |
| L5 | ~5 min beats longer on avg % viewed for this channel | Track duration vs retention | open |
