# Long-form content pillars (approved by Joe 2026-11-03)
All pillars share one identity: MrYellowScarf stickman art, narration, strong hooks, escalation, satisfying payoff. Length: ~5 min for every pillar (Joe, 2026-11-03: "keep videos 5 mins"; story may run a little over/under, never a different target).
PREMISE FIRST: never start from a topic ("history of dating"); start from a premise a stranger would click cold ("Netflix offered to sell itself. Blockbuster said no."). Reject ideas that only sound interesting after someone explains them.

| # | Pillar | Premise patterns | Verification rule | Candidate competitors (UNVERIFIED mapping; confirm by watching) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POV / experience another life | A viewer-as-protagonist with decisions, problems, consequences and progression; a start state and a changed end state; price tag or clock in first scene | Period/professional detail checked; label dramatization; not an explainer with "POV" in the title | Arnie Finance, Ranks, Paint Professor, Legend POV, Nate's Money Map, Ink Story |
| 2 | Why humans invented / how things started | Unexpected origin of a familiar thing; a strange problem and a surprising decision; a character | Origin claims are often legend: verify with primary/academic sources; mark disputed origins | Relic Explains, Quill, Draz, Ink Explainer, Lore Explained |
| 3 | What if / impossible scenarios | Universal "I've always wondered" question; escalating consequences, not a list; hypothetical assumptions stated, real facts kept real | Separate hypothetical from fact; check every real number used | OminiAM, Awaken |
| 4 | Unbelievable real stories | Sounds fictional in one sentence; real people, escalation, twist, payoff; any country/era | v2 pipeline: >= 2 independent sources, disputed parts flagged | (to discover) |
| 5 | Business / money / wealth / power | A specific question or conflict (not a biography): rivalry, bad decision, scheme, collapse, how a business really makes money; explained with visuals, no jargon | Verify figures, dates, ownership, transactions, company decisions; primary filings/major journalism | CEOzoned, Built On You, Logical Money, Arnie Finance |
| 6 | Romance / sexuality / scandal / human behavior | Concrete story with a person, stakes and a twist, not "the history of X"; attraction must add real value to the story | Verify events; allegation vs established finding; no invented intimate details/dialogue; no speculation about private sex lives as fact; historical context for sex work | Monk Mode, Day One, Cave Crush, Human Origins (taboo/body/relationship angles) + discover |

## Pillar notes
- Pillar 5 is high priority: stories with characters, ambition, competition and consequences; explain financial concepts in simple words with visual comparisons; avoid lists of company facts and jargon.
- Pillar 6: follow `character-and-attraction-policy.md` for every concept; add a separate note per idea explaining how it can be attractive and provocative without explicit content or misleading packaging.
- A famous subject does not make a good video; an obscure one can if the premise is extraordinary.
