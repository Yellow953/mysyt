# Competitors: the source of every idea
Single source of truth for competitor channels and their proven videos. Stage A (`viral-research`) refreshes it; Stage B (`script-writer`) builds ideas ONLY from rows here.
Seeded 2026-10-31 by moving data out of research/short-form/2026-10-27..10-31 notes (no new numbers). Every number carries the date it was SEEN. Blank = not recorded; never fill a blank by guessing.

**Columns**
- `seen`: date the numbers were read. `age`: video age on that date.
- `vs norm`: views ÷ the channel's typical recent views (only when we sampled them). `vs subs`: views ÷ subscribers (a weaker proxy, used when no norm sample exists). Never mix the two.
- `fmt`: S = Short, L = long-form (minutes). A long-form hit proves the CONCEPT has demand, not that it works as a Short.
- In the analysis columns, **obs:** = seen in the video/transcript/page, **hyp:** = our guess at why it worked.
