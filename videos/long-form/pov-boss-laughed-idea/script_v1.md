Title: POV: Your Boss Laughed at Your Idea — 5 Years Later, He's in Your Waiting Room

Status: FINAL for production (Joe, 2026-11-12: full autonomy). Fiction POV. Every event is [dramatization], and every money figure is invented to be believable.

Narration: 627 words. Runtime about 5:15 at Grady's ~2 words/s including pauses (estimate, re-time from the recorded clips).
Rows: 38 (one per voice clip). Every row is 4.5 s or longer (10+ words).
Illustrated (ILL, whole image): 17 of 38 rows, about 45%. Cut-out (CUT, mascot and props on a background): 21 rows.
Captions only. No on-screen text, no overlays, no disclaimers in the video. The fiction note goes in the description and pinned comment.
Music: NEW track(s) from the YouTube Audio Library, not in used.json. Cues: "bed" = sparse, "tension" = low minor, "lift" = warm resolve.

Story spine: You get laughed out of a meeting on Monday, fired on Friday, and build the clip in a garage. It grows into a grocery order, then cracks in a cold January. Five years later his failing company is the one buying from you, and he sits in your waiting room asking for a discount. You buy the company, keep the workers, and he's out.
