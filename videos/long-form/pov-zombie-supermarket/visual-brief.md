# Visual brief: pov-zombie-supermarket (2026-11-17)
- Look: style v4 bright expressive cartoon, 16:9, bright daylight everywhere (lights-out = soft blue but clearly readable, bgDim / i15). Scarf is the only yellow. No on-screen text except captions. No readable text on props: icons only (map = lines and arrows).
- Cast: "you" = the stick-figure mascot (no costume; red backpack from Level 4 on, baseball bat in Level 3). Crush = clearly adult woman, long wavy dark-brown hair, teal jacket over white top, blue jeans, white sneakers (tier 1 romance). Zombies = goofy green cartoon zombies in tattered clothes (office shirt, apron, dress, security uniform, hoodie, pajamas). NO blood, NO gore, no biting: the "eating someone" beat is a zombie hunched behind an overturned cart while shoppers scatter (i05).
- Structure (50/50): 27 ILL rows (whole illustrations, 1-4 camera crops per row, i01..i51, row 50 reuses i49) alternate with 24 CUT rows (bright background + moving cut-out sprites, icons: heart, ex, moon, bulb, home, map).
- Levels: Outbreak (rows 1-9), L1 Shelter (10-15), L2 Fortress (16-21), L3 First attack (22-29), L4 Running out of time (30-37), L5 Escape (38-44), Twist (45-51). Headings are not voiced or shown.
- Sheets: A mascot extras (run, scared, bat, sneak, backpack, drive), B crush (8 poses), C zombies (6), D props/people (cart, shelf, supplies, van, 2 shoppers, generator, crates). Backgrounds: bgAisle, bgEntrance, bgStorage, bgDim, bgLoading, bgParking, bgRoad.
