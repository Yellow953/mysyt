## L. Myths and traps (do not say these)

1. "The kidneys can't make urine saltier than seawater." Literal version is borderline false (see A2). Use the nuance.
2. "Nesbitt, 1942, Lancet." Wrong; Ladell, 1943 (A7).
3. "Saudi Arabia's first plant was in the 1920s." Not supported (D3).
4. "Dubai's first plant was in the 1960s." Not found (D5). Abu Dhabi 1962 is supported (D4).
5. "IEA says the GCC has 33% of world desalination." Not found (F1).
6. "Hassyan is the world's largest and is running." Under construction, not commissioned (F6).
7. "Ras Al-Khair is the world's largest." Disputed (F5).
8. "Desal is 100% of Gulf water." False. Desalination is 18% to 77% of total supply by country, and groundwater dominates in Oman, Saudi and UAE (F8, Al Jazeera).
9. "Jones et al. in Environmental Science & Technology." Wrong journal; it is Science of the Total Environment (G1).
10. "Thermal energy kWh vs RO kWh is a like-for-like comparison." It is not, unless heat is converted (E5).
11. "334 L/day per person is how much Gulf people use." It is desalinated output per person, not use (I2).
12. "Israel's plant shutdown is a Gulf story." It is Mediterranean (K1).
