## Premise
Part 1, The army: ex-WWI soldier-settlers on marginal WA wheat land, hit by the Depression and falling prices, get machine guns from the army against emus. The government's condition: the farmers pay for the bullets, bunks and bread. The army fights for 38 days and pulls out.
Part 2, The bounty: the army's failure does not end it. The emus were protected by law, then listed as vermin, then paid for per beak. The gunner ends up filing claims. The count is disputed, the system gets gamed, and the emus are still here.

One-line thesis for the script: the gun went home; the paperwork kept going, and it got cheated.
