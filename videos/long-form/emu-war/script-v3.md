## Self-review

**Weakest three moments**
1. **Scenes 20, 22b, 42 (the three soft beats).** Scene 20 is a single-source nickname with no story job. Scene 42 is a refusal claim from one source that sits right before the 1943 payoff. Scene 22b repeats the "count is disputed" point that 22a already makes.
2. **Scenes 22a to 23 (number stack).** Four figures in 25 seconds (986, 9,860 rounds, settler count, "crawled off") right before the turn. Attention dips here.
3. **Scenes 45a and 45b (last hedge).** "As far as I can tell" carries the ending, and its status claim has no current source. If the source is not found, the line has to be cut, and the ending gets weaker.

**What to cut first, in order:** scene 20 (saves 14 words, about 6 s), scene 42 (saves 8 words, about 5 s; then 41 keeps only "Not the army"), scene 22b (saves 9 words, about 5 s). Those three cuts take the script to about 650 words and 5:25. To reach 5:00 at Grady's pace, also drop scene 7a or merge 7a into 7b.
