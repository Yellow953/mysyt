## Self-review

**Weakest three moments**
1. **Scene 24 ("Wrong answer.").** The bridge is clean, but a dry read can sound like a joke about the law rather than a turn. Check the delivery in the first take.
2. **Scene 42 (gaming).** Undated. It reads as "later" only because it follows the 1947 line. The source places it in the 1961-73 tallies. It is the weakest chronology link.
3. **Scene 35 (gunner's stake).** Nine seconds on one hypothetical line and a map shot. If it lands soft, keep the line and cut the visual beat, not the quote.

**What to cut first, in order:** scene 42 (gaming, 6 s), then scene 30 ("Years on", 8 s). Do not cut 38 (refusals), 44 (gun), 46-47 (payoff) or 35 (peak).

**Claims to double-check before any render**
- Game Act 1874 and the June to September offence (24). Quoted by S4, statute not opened.
- "Mid-1930s" for the 57,034 count (31). S4 says Northampton invasions commencing 1935, S1 says 1934. Keep the "one count says" hedge; no other year.
- 1943 "added free ammunition" (36). Research B13 says "switched from bonuses," but S4 bonus tables continue to 1945-60, so the script does not use "switched." Confirm.
- Army requests and refusals (37, 38, 44). S1 is a Wikipedia lead, not a source of record.
- Depression, falling prices, and the hold-back threat (6). S1 only.
- Scene 35 quote, with "the Game Act" restored. Check the printed S4 text.
- Scene 23 "The birds didn't." Reads as general; no current wildlife source.
- Scene 30 "still farming." Composite gunner, invented.
- Scene 1 "about a thousand emus" and scene 15 "about fifty seen, perhaps a dozen dead." Verified, but the kill count varies by source.
- Thumbnail text "FARMERS PAID" (packaging) is a separate decision from the video's no-overlay rule. Confirm with Joe.
