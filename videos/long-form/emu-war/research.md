## Myths to avoid
- "Army lost to emus" as settled fact. It is a popular framing; accounts differ.
- "Emus killed by machine gun / 986 is proven". 986 is Meredith's own tally, disputed.
- "2,500 emus wounded and died" as fact. Disputed, Meredith's claim.
- "2,500 rounds = 2,500 kills" or "10,000 rounds = 10,000 emus". Rounds fired are not kills.
- "Meredith was a Lieutenant-Colonel" or any rank other than Major. Not found.
- "Later army culls in 1934/1943/1948". Requests refused; bounties used.
- "The PM stopped it". S1 and S2 say Pearce's government (S3 says prime minister). Use "the government".
- "Ten thousand emus killed". Not supported.
- "Each mob has a leader" quote. Not found.

## Surprising details (sourced)
- Pearce's nickname was "Minister for the Emu War" (S2).
- Emus were judged to have "won every round so far" in parliament (S2, S3).
- A Fox Movietone cameraman filmed the operation (S3).
- Meredith's report said many more birds had crawled off to die unseen (S3).
- A 1928 cull killed over 3,000 emus (S3).
- Bounties, not soldiers, did most of the work later: 57,034 claims in six months, 1934 (S1).

## Story lines the script can use (honest)
- "By most accounts, about 20,000 emus..." (not a census).
- "Meredith's report counted 986 birds dead from 9,860 rounds. Settlers and other accounts put the figure far lower, and Meredith himself said many more had crawled off to die."
- "Accounts quote him as saying a military division with that bullet-carrying capacity would face any army in the world." (hedged wording)

## Confidence
Medium-high on the core arc (causes, dates, weapons, jam, withdrawal, return, end, bounty). Medium on exact numbers (all disputed). Low on quote wording and on McMurray/O'Halloran ranks (single source). Johnson 2006 full text was not accessed; read it before any figure goes in a final script.

**Best facts for a 2.5 min story (all from S4 unless stated)**
1. 1874: emus protected by law. 1922: listed as vermin. Farmers paid per beak.
2. Proof = beak. Shire tallies. 1 shilling a head (1945-60).
3. Northampton season: 57,034 birds destroyed Aug-Jan, north of No.3 fence; vs army's 986 claimed (S5 says "highly disputed").
4. 1943 quote: "virtually lost control".
5. Gaming: birds shot outside the fence claimed inside.
6. End: 284,724 beaks 1945-60; 162,353 birds 1961-73 (52,515 + 109,838, "cannot be relied upon").
