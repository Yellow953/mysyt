## Top 5 problems (exact locations)
1. **Scene 3, 0:16-0:24 (hook zone): "He's imagined, but the job was real."** This is a spoken disclaimer, which breaks production rule 2. Reframe in character: "Imagine a gunner. Let's call him Mr. Yellow Scarf."
2. **Scenes 25 to 26 (2:54-3:06): broken logic at the Part 2 turn.** Scene 25 asks "So what finally worked?" and scene 26 answers "The law had come first" with the 1874 Game Act, which protected emus and did not work. The viewer loses the thread. Add one bridge line: "The first answer was the law, and it made things worse."
3. **Part 2 timeline scrambles (scenes 32 to 44).** The sequence runs 1945-60 (32), then 1932 (33), then an undated count (34a, 34b), then 1961-73 (37), 1943 (38-40), 1947 (43), 1945-60 again (44). The 57,034 figure has no date anchor. Fix: "In the mid-1930s, one count says..." (covers 1934 and 1935 without picking one) and drop "Across those years" in 44 in favour of "Between 1945 and 1960".
4. **Scene 45b, 5:12-5:22: "And the birds, as far as I can tell, never left."** No current source exists in research.md (it says protection and tallies only to 1973). This breaks the research limit and is the ending's payoff. Cut it, or source a current wildlife-authority status before any render. Minimum fallback: "The tallies run to 1973, and nobody counted them to zero."
5. **Unhedged single-source lines (S1 Wikipedia lead only):** scene 7a "Farmers threatened to hold back their harvest" (no hedge); scene 7b "the drought pushed the emus..." (causal, unhedged); scene 41 "Farmers had asked for the army back more than once" (no hedge). Add "accounts say" or "by most accounts" to each.

Also flagged, lower rank:
- **Scene 39 quote trim.** Research has "...the conservation law-The Game Act-have virtually lost control." The script drops "The Game Act" silently. Keep the printed wording (three words) or use an ellipsis in the caption. Do not trim silently.
- **Scene 23, "many more had crawled off to die."** Single source (S3). It sits next to the banned "2,500 wounded birds dying" myth. Keep only as "Meredith's report says..." with the hedge.
- **Scene 42 vs scene 45a.** The script's self-review says cut 42 first. Do not. Scene 45a ("The gun never came back") rests on the refusal claims in 41-42. Cut 22b and the other fillers instead.
- **Scene 47 "He reaches for the pencil."** Reads as the gunner returning to bounty work, which is the complicity the story condemns. That may be the intended sting, but it is not clear. Make it explicit in 46 or 47 (for example, "He picks up the pencil. The emus keep walking.") or cut 47.
