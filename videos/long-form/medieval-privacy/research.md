## Popular myths vs documented facts
- **Myth:** "Everyone slept with the animals." **Fact:** Some regions (Dartmoor longhouses) yes; others no (#1, #2).
- **Myth:** "Medieval people had no concept of privacy at all." **Fact:** Scholars now say privacy was limited and uneven, with solars, inner rooms and beds as partial answers (#13).
- **Myth:** "Bathhouses were brothels." **Fact:** Southwark stews were licensed brothels; most bathhouses were ordinary (#15, #16).
- **Myth:** "Witnesses fingered the bride's stocking." **Fact:** Not documented in anything I found (#19).
- **Myth:** "Medieval people slept sitting up because lying down meant death." **Fact:** Folklore (#21).
