## 2. Description (paste as is)
```
One room, a whole family, and a village that wants to walk you to bed. Where do two newlyweds go to get five minutes alone?

This is a comedic dramatization. The couple, the family and every plan are invented. The history is simplified and hedged: guests walked some couples to bed in some places, wealthier households had private upper rooms, church rules on when couples could be together existed but how strictly they were kept is debated, and box beds gave some enclosure in crowded homes.

Medieval privacy in a one-room house: where couples could get a moment alone, from wedding-night guests to upstairs rooms.
Medieval wedding night customs and newlywed life, told as a story, with real history woven into the jokes.
Box beds in medieval homes: the wooden bed with walls and a door that gave some privacy in a crowded house.
```
