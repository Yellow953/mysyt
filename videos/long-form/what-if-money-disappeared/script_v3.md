# What If Money Disappeared Overnight? (script v3, 2026-11-14)

Status: DRAFT v3 for Joe's approval. Nothing produced yet. v1 and v2 kept as script_v1.md and script_v2.md.
Source: Joe's ChatGPT script (2026-11-14), adapted to our format: one voice clip per row, Mr. Yellow Scarf as "you" with a little sarcastic humour, flat ink visuals (at most 3 characters and 1-2 props per image, no screens or signs with content).

What we kept from the ChatGPT script (why it works):
- It opens on a paradox, not an intro: everything still exists (food, houses, factories), so why would the world collapse? Plus a second open question: would this make everyone equal?
- One chain of consequences, each chapter caused by the one before: first morning, barter fails, supply chains break, a new kind of rich, humanity rebuilds. It escalates from one person to a supermarket, a factory, a hospital and then society.
- Concrete examples anyone can follow (farmer, doctor, shoemaker) and a surprise reversal (billionaire with an empty fridge, the farmer richest on the street).
- The ending reframes the premise: money was never the value, only the system that exchanged it.
What we changed: shorter spoken lines, the mascot lives the story, humour in his voice, the one hedged real anchor (WW2 camp cigarettes), and a callback joke in the last row.

Narration about 640 words, 37 rows, about 4:55. Captions are the only on-screen text. Fiction/what-if; one real anchor, hedged.
Music: one calm, mysterious instrumental bed that lifts at the "rebuilding" chapter.
