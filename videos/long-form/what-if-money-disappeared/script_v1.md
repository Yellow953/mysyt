## Setups and payoffs (checked)
- Card declined (1) to card refused as deposit (10) to card as shim (17) to card approved (33, 34, 38). Last line echoes the hook.
- "Barista laughs at your cash" (2) to "still laughing" (32). Her laugh at the start is the one she keeps.
- Bad overnight update (3) to "nobody ever agreed on the cause" (35). The cause is never answered, only shrugged at.
- Gas light (6) to "car stays parked" (11). Pump and jet ski set up in row 7.
- Earbuds (8) to earbuds back in pocket (9). Bread wins; the earbuds were never the trade.
- Rent (5) to landlord note with "undecided" (11) to "eggs, then" (18). Rent loop closes at the carton.
- Jet ski man (7) to jet ski man at the coop (13) to jet ski under a tarp, owes four weeks (29). Same guy in all three.
- Your TV (13) to TV in the neighbor's shed (29).
- Rubber band (15) to snap (16). Card shim (17) to the card at the end.
- Cigarettes as change (19) to POW camp money (20) to "nobody else's" notes (22). Cigarettes are the earlier money; they are not reused.
- Signed note (21) to "who signs" (23) to ledger (24, 31, 35). Neighbor holds the ledger at the end.
- Egg loan (25) to owe three dozen (26) to rival lecture (28). Eggs are the currency motif all the way to the CTA (37).
- Glass breaks (30) to new padlock (31). Implied only.
- Zero (4) to "Money was never the paper" (36). The zero is never explained, by design.

## Self-review
Weakest three moments:

2. Row 16, the rubber band gag. The setup is strong, but the visual needs to sell the snap. If the animation can't do it, drop "four eggs" and keep the snap.
3. Row 26 and row 28, the Year One stretch. Two of the four Year One rows are about the same debt. Row 26 is the cut to make first if the runtime runs long (about 6 s).

What to cut first if the cut runs over: row 26 (owe three dozen), then row 6 (gas light). Keep row 11, because it closes the gas light and the rent note together.
