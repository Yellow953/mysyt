# What If Every Bank Account on Earth Hit Zero? (script v2, 2026-11-14)

Status: DRAFT v2 for Joe's approval. Nothing produced yet. v1 is kept as script_v1.md.
Why v2 (Joe, 2026-11-13: "the story is weird and not very understandable"): v1 jumped between random gags (jet ski, rubber band, birdseed, a ledger, a rewind) with no visible cause and effect, and it never explained why anything became money. v2 is one straight line, told in time order, no rewinds. Every step answers "why does this happen next?" and the whole video teaches one idea: money only works because people trust it.

Narration about 560 words, 30 rows (one voice clip each), about 4:45. Captions are the only on-screen text. Fiction, with one real anchor (WW2 prison-camp cigarettes, hedged).
Music: one calm, curious instrumental bed.

Story spine: your card is declined. Nothing is wrong with the goods, only the trust. Cash is useless because nobody will accept it. You try swapping goods, and it fails because both sides must want what the other has. The street invents money (bottle caps), a cheater floods it, then people trust one honest baker's notes instead, and she ends up inventing a bank. Ten years later the numbers come back and everyone just believes again. Payoff: money is an agreement everyone believes.

Visual rules for the redo: flat hand-drawn ink style like the medieval-privacy and seawater videos (not the glossy painted look), at most 3 characters and 1 or 2 props per image, no screens or signs with content (icons only), simple stick-figure mascot.

## Check list
- Rewind removed. No wife, jet ski, rubber band, birdseed or ledger.
- Every step has a reason: no trust, so shops close; no matching wants, so swaps fail; so caps; cheaters flood caps; so trust moves to a person; so notes; so a bank.
- Setups pay off: the egg and onion (7), the TV (8, 10), the goat (10, 11), the café (1, 26).
- No wording like "worst deal". No text on props.
