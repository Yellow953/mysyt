## Description
Imagine waking up tomorrow and every dollar in the world has disappeared. The food, the houses, the factories and the hospitals are all still there, so why would the world collapse? And would it finally make everyone equal? Follow the chain reaction: the empty supermarket checkout, the barter nightmare of the farmer, the doctor and the shoemaker, the factories and trucks that stop, the people who suddenly matter most, and what money really was all along.

This is an imagined what-if thought experiment, told for fun with a cartoon character. Illustration only, not financial advice or a prediction.

What would YOU do in a world without money? Tell me in the comments.
