## Visual: one worked example (HYPOTHETICAL, label on screen "illustration, not real data")
Use round numbers. A restaurant sells $1,000,000 of burgers a year.
- Old model (Kroc's early deal): the company takes a small percentage of sales as a fee. Illustration: 2% = $20,000 a year, from which it must run the whole company.
- Landlord model: the company leases the building and ground for, say, $60,000 a year and subleases it to the franchisee for $75,000 a year (a 25% markup, invented). Plus a royalty of 4% of sales, $40,000. Franchisee's rent can also rise with sales (percent-of-sales rent, as the 10-K confirms exists).
- Picture: a burger on the left, a pipe of coins running through a "landlord" tollbooth. Two piggy banks: "royalty" and "rent." Real scale, FY2025: rent $10.4B vs royalty $6.0B.
- Do not claim the real royalty or rent percentages. The 10-K does not give them in what I read.

## What NOT to state
1. "Sonneborn said it at Wharton in 1974" (unfound).
2. "McDonald's makes X% of profit from rent" (10-K does not split).
3. Specific markup percentages (20%/40%) or "5% of sales whichever higher" (low-quality sources).
4. The handshake royalty as a fact, or any dollar estimate of what the brothers lost.
5. "Kroc cheated/betrayed the brothers" (Dick said no regrets; contested).
6. That the The Founder quote is Sonneborn's real words.
7. That McDonald's owns 56% of all land worldwide (it is consolidated markets).
8. 43,477 restaurants unless FY2024 10-K is opened. Use 45,356 (FY2025).
9. Sonneborn's exact hire date and title years (sources conflict).
10. Exact creation date of Franchise Realty (1955/1956/1957 across sources).

## Story-spine notes
See the report to the coordinator. Short form: Kroc is a salesman with a thin fee; Sonneborn is the finance man who changes the question from "how many burgers" to "who owns the ground"; conflict with the McDonald brothers who refuse to expand (Kroc buys them out for $2.7M); twist: the famous quote is movie dialogue, but the numbers are real (rent $10.4B vs royalties $6.0B in FY2025); coda: Sonneborn left in 1967 and is largely forgotten.

## Confidence
High on today's 10-K structure and land/building percentages. Medium on history (secondary sources). Low on the Sonneborn quote's origin and the handshake royalty.

## Direct read of Franchise Arrangements note (2026-11-04)
Source: McDonald's FY2025 Form 10-K (mcd-20251231.htm), "Franchise Arrangements" footnote in the Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements (10-K p.53; the notes are titled by name, not numbered). Table: "Revenues from franchised restaurants consisted of" ($ millions, 2025 / 2024 / 2023):
