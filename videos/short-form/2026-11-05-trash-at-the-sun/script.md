# Why don't we fill a rocket with our trash and send it into the Sun? (cut-out batch 1/4, H8)
Status: PRODUCED 2026-11-08 (v2, rocket FILLED with trash). Final cut in output/. Not uploaded.

## Script (~85 words, est. ~42 s with Grady; plain words, no "!")
1. Why don't we just fill a rocket with our trash and dump it in the Sun?
2. The Sun is huge. Hard to miss.
3. But Earth moves sideways at thirty kilometers a second, and the rocket keeps that speed. So it misses, and circles the Sun.
4. To actually hit it, you have to cancel that speed. That takes fifty-five times more energy than going to Mars.
5. And we make over two billion tonnes of trash a year. That's at least eighty million full rockets. Every year.
6. So, recycle.
