## Requirements (Joe's)
- **Mix:** real cut-out animation (poses swap with squash/anticipation, props move, effects are sprites, camera eased, slide transitions in voice gaps) AND at least ONE whole illustrated scene image (full-frame, style v4 bright expressive cartoon, glossy colors, bright sky or sunset, thin dark outlines, exaggerated emotion; use the Mr Yellow Scarf element tag `<<<aa6661ee-d997-489c-9e1b-9b9adbed8c94>>>`; scarf is the only yellow; "only the main character wears a scarf"; no text/digits in the image; gpt_image_2 low/1k, 0.5 credit; vertical 9:16 or pan across a wider image at full height). Use the whole image where it gives the most punch (usually the hook or the big reveal) and Ken Burns it. More than one whole image is fine if the budget allows.
- **Strong hook:** the first 3-5 s show the premise in ONE line over the most striking, readable-on-a-phone image at frame 0 (the viewer decides in about a second), then a punch (zoom punch + thud on the key word). Frame 0 must already show the question visually. If Grady lands the key word after ~4 s, speed the hook clip ~1.15x.
- **Thumbnail:** make an interesting designed cover, not a random frame: one big emotion, a clear focal figure, contrast, 2-3 words of text that ADD to the title (drawn locally with `scripts/short-form/thumbnail.py` or Pillow, Impact yellow with black outline, text between 12% and 88% of the height, not over faces). Prefer the whole illustrated image (design its prompt so it works as the cover too: big single emotion, empty space for text) so no extra credits are spent; make 2 variants at most. Check at 270 px wide.
- **Loop:** last frame slides back to frame 0.
- **Positioning rules (Joe, volcano round 1-2):** character fully inside frame with margins, ground drawn in FRONT of set-piece bases, objects sit on the ground (ground line ~73% of height, captions below), character big (head radius ~115 px), effects (smoke, fire, splash) are generated sprites, not code circles; no stacking of labels on faces. Check every beat on a contact sheet at phone size before the final render.
- **Smoothness:** 60 fps; anticipation squash then stretch on every pose swap; no hard cuts between locations.
- **Voice:** Grady (Higgsfield text2speech_v2, variant elevenlabs, preset voice_id e2a2d2e6-9ed2-59cd-82af-feaa27f8a678), one clip per script line, plain delivery, no "!" or hype words; pauses after each (0.3-0.7 s, longer before punchlines). Send voice clips 8 at a time; resubmit any 429s. If total runs over target length, trim pauses first, then atempo up to 1.12. Set caption chunks from the real clip times / RMS pauses (faster_whisper is NOT installed).
- **SFX:** synthesize with `scripts/short-form/make_sfx.py` (whoosh, pop, sizzle, thud, clink, cough, rumble, boing; add new ones if needed), synced to actions, big sound inside a pause.
- **Music:** a different track from the previous videos and from the other three in this batch. Choose from tracks ALREADY in `assets/music/` (check `assets/music/used.json` and the other videos' choices; do not download anything new, that needs Joe's OK). Quiet bed ~0.22, ducked under the voice, loudnorm to about -14 LUFS.
- **Facts:** before rendering, do the primary-source checks listed in your claims table (WebSearch/WebFetch; prefer NASA, NOAA, USGS, WHO, official government/utility pages). Update the claims table in script.md with the result. If a claim fails or can only be sourced weakly, soften the wording in the script (and tell the parent), never keep a claim you could not support. Jokes may exaggerate, facts may not. Keep the approved script wording otherwise; any change goes in script.md under "Changes vs approved".
- **Monetization-safe:** no nudity, gore or graphic violence; cartoon only.
