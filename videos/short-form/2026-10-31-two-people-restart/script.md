## Facts check (research.md)
- Habsburg: 29.4% died before 1 and 50% before 10 among 34 royal children (1527-1661); Charles II died 1700 childless. We say "Spain's Habsburg kings... half their children died before ten" = royal families sample; hedge kept as "Half".
- Pingelap: 1770s typhoon, about 20 survivors, hidden recessive gene, up to roughly one in ten affected (sources 5-10%).
- Pitcairn: 27 settlers in 1790; by 1800 Adams was the last mutineer, women and children remained; the men died mainly from murder, drink and illness. Our line "killed by each other, not by genes" is the simplification of "alcoholism, murder, disease"; consider adding "mostly" if Joe wants: "mostly by each other".
- "Eight billion" = current world population (rounded).
- "Almost certainly not" / "twenty did it" / "find eighteen more": hedged opinion; no minimum population is claimed as a number.
