2. Everyone's heard it. So he circles the calendar and waits.

## Self-review (Stage G, as a critic)
- Hook: premise is visible at frame 0 (gulp + "7 YEARS") and the line states the myth so everyone nods before the twist. Weak spot was a bare myth-bust; the mascot's calendar bet (line 2, 7) gives it a small story arc and the loop pays it off.
- One gag per line, gags are visual (cobweb, bent scissors, corn waving bye); story works without them: myth, why it feels true, why it leaves, rare caveat.
- Twist (corn) lands in line 4, right after the "scary" line 3. Honest hedge in line 6 keeps trust.
- Plain wording, no "!", no unsourced claims, no origin story.
