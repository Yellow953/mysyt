Think pirates spent all day fighting, stealing treasure, and drinking rum? Yeah... not even close. Most of the time, being a pirate was basically the worst job at sea. They hauled ropes and mended sails. Miss your shift? Congratulations. You're everyone's least favorite pirate. And the food? Hard biscuits. Fresh fruit? Basically a luxury. But when they spotted a merchant ship? EVERYTHING changed. Weapons out, hoping the other ship surrendered. Win, and they got money, supplies, and cargo. Lose, and their career ended VERY badly. So yeah, pirate life wasn't nonstop treasure hunting. It was mostly sea chores... with occasional murder attempts. Still wanna be a pirate?
