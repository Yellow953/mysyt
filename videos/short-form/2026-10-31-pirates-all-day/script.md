## Facts (light check, jokes exaggerate)
Pirate crews did constant ship upkeep (sails, rigging, lookout); food was mostly hard biscuit and salted meat with little fresh fruit; pirates often hoped targets would surrender; winners shared loot, losers risked capture or death (average career often cited as about two years). Sources: https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1835 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirate_code · https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/foods-pirates-ate-sea . Description caveat: "worst job at sea" is a joke; some sources say pirates ate and were treated better than navy sailors.
