It's 1554. You're opening a Starbucks in Istanbul. No Wi-Fi. No logo. Just coffee, backgammon, and comfy cushions. Poets show up. Then scholars. People call it the School of the Wise. You sell one coffee. They stay five hours. Soon the whole city copies you. Hundreds of coffeehouses. Then the Sultan checks who's inside. Soldiers. Plotting. In 1633, Murad IV shuts every coffeehouse in Istanbul. You built a place so good at getting people talking, it scared an empire. Later, the coffeehouses come back. Your Starbucks lasted seventy-nine years. The idea? Still open.
