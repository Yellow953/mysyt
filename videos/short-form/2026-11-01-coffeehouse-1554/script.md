# Script: "What If You Opened a Starbucks in 1554 Istanbul?"
Batch 1/3 of style test "Modern person in the past". Source concept: Originico "rich selling burgers in ancient Rome" 4.2M, BoneHustle chef in medieval times 1.8M (competitors.md 2b, Shorts). Kept: modern business model dropped into the past, "you" in it. New: a place, not a product; the ending is the Sultan, not the money.

**Story spine (outline merged because Joe asked for the script directly):** Who: you (lazy everyman with a coffee shop idea). Want: get rich. Conflict: the idea works too well. Turn: the Sultan sees who is sitting inside. Payoff: you built a place so good at getting people talking it scared an empire, and it outlived the Sultan.
**Hook/first frame:** "It's 1554. You're opening a Starbucks in Istanbul." Mr. Yellow Scarf in a plain green apron behind a wooden counter, steaming cups, an Ottoman stone arch behind him, overlay "ISTANBUL 1554". Co-star: no.
**Emotion:** curiosity, delight, then a small jolt of dread, then a satisfied "of course".
