It's April 1848. Nobody knows about the gold yet. You do. Everyone's about to dig. You hate digging. So you buy every pan you can find. Twenty cents each. May 12. You sprint through San Francisco, waving a bottle of gold dust. Gold! Gold from the American River! Pans now cost fifteen dollars. That's seventy-five times what you paid. The miners dig for years. Most never get rich. You never touch a shovel. You just count. A man named Sam Brannan really did this. California's first millionaire. Then he lost it all. You read ahead.
