# Script: "Could You Get Rich in the GOLD RUSH Without Digging?"
Batch 1/3 of style test "Modern person in the past". Source concept: Originico "rich selling burgers in ancient Rome" 4.2M; MR_DATA / Organic Engine "you in the past" Shorts (competitors.md 2b). Kept: money + hindsight + you in it. New: the lazy everyman refuses to dig; the real man who did it (Sam Brannan) gives the ending.

**Story spine:** Who: you, who hate digging. Want: gold without the work. Conflict: you must sell the idea before anyone else sells the pans. Turn: one run through San Francisco makes pans 75x more expensive. Payoff: you never touch a shovel; and Brannan, who did this for real, lost it all, but you read ahead.
**Hook/first frame:** "It's April 1848. Nobody knows about the gold yet. You do." Mascot lounging on a heap of tin pans, hands behind his head, tiny miners wading in a river far behind. Overlay "APRIL 1848". Co-star: no.
**Emotion:** lazy smugness, comic sprint, dry bite at the end.
