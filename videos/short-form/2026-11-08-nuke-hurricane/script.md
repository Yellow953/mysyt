# Why don't we just nuke a hurricane? (cut-out batch 2/4, H8)
Status: PRODUCED 2026-11-08 (not uploaded). ~5.3 credits.

## Script (~52 words, est. ~27 s with Grady; plain words, no "!")
1. Why don't we just nuke a hurricane?
2. Seems fair. Big storm, bigger bomb.
3. Except a hurricane releases heat equal to a ten-megaton bomb every twenty minutes.
4. So your bomb is a tiny firework inside a giant spinning storm.
5. And now the fallout drifts onto land.
6. So, no. Just evacuate.

## Self-review
- Hook: question + a huge visual contrast (tiny bomb vs giant storm). Twist: the bomb is the small thing. Payoff: radioactive storm + "just evacuate". Weakest: line 3 is a number; the repeating bomb icons must carry it.

## Changes vs approved (2026-11-08)
- Line 3: "the energy of" -> "heat equal to" (NOAA says the HEAT release equals the bomb).
- Line 5: "And now the storm is radioactive." -> "And now the fallout drifts onto land." (NOAA says fallout drifts with trade winds to land; the storm itself is not claimed radioactive). Beat 5 visual: green fallout clouds drifting toward a coast.
- On-screen label during line 3: "10-MEGATON BOMB / EVERY 20 MIN".
