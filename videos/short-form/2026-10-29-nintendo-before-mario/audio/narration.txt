Before Mario, Nintendo ran a taxi company. Also instant rice. Also a vacuum cleaner. Then its share price fell from nine hundred yen to sixty. So how did this mess end up making your childhood? It started with flower cards. Yes, cards. 1889, by their own count. Then Disney cards sold at least six hundred thousand packs. You'd have retired. Instead, in the 1960s: taxis. Union trouble. Sold. Then a maintenance guy built a toy arm for fun. It sold. Fifteen years later, same company: Donkey Kong. Mario's debut. You'd still be driving a taxi. Which side hustle would you have tried?
