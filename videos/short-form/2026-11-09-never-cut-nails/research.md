## Notes
- Strongest honest angle: the answer to "what if you never cut them" is not "gross claws"; it is "a man actually did it for 66 years and it cost him his hand".
- Important correction to the brief: the world record was grown on ONE hand, deliberately, with that hand rendered unusable. Normal uncut nails do not reach this: they snap. Hedge any statement on the break point.
- Math if used: 909.6 cm / 66 yr = ~13.8 cm a year, ~1.15 cm a month, i.e. ~11.5 mm a month summed over 5 nails = ~2.3 mm/nail/month. This is consistent with the ~3 mm figure (our arithmetic; do not state).
- Sources: Guinness (guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2018/7/owner-of-worlds-longest-nails-has-them-cut-after-growing-them-for-66-years-532563), NPR 2018-07-13, Ripley's press page, hero.epa.gov ref 7330295.
