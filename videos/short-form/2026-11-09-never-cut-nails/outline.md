# Outline: What if you never cut your fingernails? (Stage E, 2026-11-09)

## Honesty check
Generic "never cut your nails" explainer is weak (nails just snap). The real story is stronger: one man did it on purpose for 66 years. Recommend the PERSON angle (Chillal) with the question as the hook.

## Central question
What actually happens if you never cut your nails?

## Premise
A man tried it for 66 years. He got nails longer than a bus, and lost the use of his hand.

## Mascot's stake
Mr. Yellow Scarf stars as the "you" who tries it: a lazy guy who thinks "free hack, never trim again." Worst deal: skipping a 2-minute chore costs him his hand.

## Conflict / turn
Setup: lazy shortcut ("I'm never cutting these"). Turn: it works, they get insanely long (the record) but he cannot use the hand, cannot sleep. Twist: the most extreme case ended with a saw in Times Square.

## Hook options (frame 0 + line)
1. (Recommended) Whole illustrated image: mascot with one hand holding up a nail as long as a school bus beside him, shocked. Line: "A man never cut his left-hand nails for sixty-six years."
2. "What if you never cut your fingernails?" over a split image: normal hand vs giant curled nails.
3. "Skip nail clippers for one year? Here's how it ended for one man." (softer)

## Beats (~36 s, ~72 words)
1. Hook, WHOLE IMAGE (0-4 s): premise line over the giant-nails image; punch zoom on "sixty-six years". Earns it: the most striking image + the answer up front.
2. Cut-out (4-9 s): "Your nails grow about three millimeters a month. Normally they snap long before that matters." SEE: ruler/calendar, nail growing, then snapping with a crack. Why: the normal answer, quick.
3. Cut-out (9-15 s): "Shridhar Chillal, India, decided at fourteen to stop, after a teacher scolded him for breaking hers." SEE: young Chillal-style kid and teacher, snap. Why: motive/story.
4. Cut-out (15-22 s): counter ticks 1952 -> 2018; nails grow past floor, past the bed; tape measure to 909.6 cm, "ABOUT 30 FT". Why: the escalation and the number.
5. Cut-out (22-30 s): cost: hand frozen in a fist, he says it hurt all the way to the shoulder, wakes every half hour to flip it. SEE: bed gag, mascot mimics. Why: the stake and turn.
6. Cut-out (30-36 s): July 2018, Times Square, a saw grinds them off; the nails go on display. Mascot's closing line. Why: payoff.

## Endings
- A (recommended, pays off hook, loops): "So yes, you can skip the clippers. He did, for sixty-six years. You'd need clippers." SEE: mascot clipping one nail with a click, slide back into frame 0 (giant nails). Loop: last frame is the nail image.
- B: "Two minutes with clippers. His price was a hand."
- C: question: "Would you grow them for a world record?" (comment bait, weaker payoff)
