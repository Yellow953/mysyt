## Script (Grady, phrase by phrase; plain wording, no "!")
1. A man never cut his left-hand nails for sixty-six years.
2. Nails grow about three millimeters a month, and usually snap long before it matters.
3. Shridhar Chillal, India, started at fourteen, after a teacher scolded him for breaking hers.
4. By 2018: almost thirty feet. A world record.
5. He says the hand locked into a fist, and he woke every half hour to move it.
6. In July 2018, a saw in Times Square cut them off.
7. So yes, skip the clippers. Just know what it cost him.

## Self-review (Stage G, as a critic)
- Hook: the answer ("a man did it for 66 years") plus the giant nail at frame 0; swipe-stopper is the number, not a question. Works as the cover.
- Story without jokes: teen decision, 66 years, cost (his hand), the saw. Complete. Jokes are visual only (mascot shock, snap, backing away).
- Respect: no line mocks Chillal or his disability; line 5 is sincere with "he says"; the mascot reacts to the nails, not the man.
