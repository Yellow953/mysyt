Every level of life in ancient Egypt. From having absolutely nothing… to being worshipped as a god. Level one: Enslaved person. You have little control over your life, and your daily reality depends heavily on who controls your labor. This is survival mode. Level two: Farmer. You work the land, grow food, and hope the Nile floods at the right time. Your boss is basically the weather. Level three: Skilled craftsman. You can make pottery, furniture, or beautiful objects. Congratulations! You have a valuable skill… and still have bills to pay. Level four: Scribe. You learned to read and write hieroglyphic and other Egyptian scripts. Suddenly, the government needs you to keep track of absolutely everything. Level five: High official. You manage resources, oversee workers, and stand close to the people running the kingdom. Your sandals are probably nicer now. Level six: Noble. You have wealth, land, influence, and a much more comfortable life. Meanwhile, everyone else is wondering how you got that house. Final level: Pharaoh. You rule the kingdom, command enormous resources, and are treated as a divine ruler. Your biggest problem? Making sure the entire country keeps running. Which level would you choose? Personally, I'd take the palace.
