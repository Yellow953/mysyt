Every level of being a businessman. From making your first five dollars… to owning a company that makes millions. Level one: The Hustler. You sell anything you can find. Old phones, sneakers, random stuff from your bedroom. Your startup capital? Basically zero. Level two: Freelancer. You finally make money from your skills. There's just one problem: if you stop working, the money stops coming. Level three: Startup Founder. You have an idea, a logo, and absolutely no customers. But don't worry. Your pitch deck says you're changing the world. Level four: Business Owner. Customers are finally paying. Now you're managing employees, invoices, angry emails, and taxes. Congratulations. You created yourself a job. Level five: CEO. You have a team, real revenue, and an actual office. You no longer do everything yourself. Instead, you attend meetings about meetings. Level six: Serial Entrepreneur. You've built multiple businesses. Money is coming from different directions, and your calendar looks more complicated than a NASA launch. Final level: Business Empire. Your companies run with entire leadership teams. You make huge decisions, invest millions, and somehow still ask your assistant where your keys are. Which level are you at right now?
