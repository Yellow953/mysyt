You join Rome's army. Two hundred twenty-five silver coins a year. Payday. In one record, food alone took a third. Boots. Tunic. Hay, maybe for the squad's mule. A shared mule. An unshared bill. Soldiers griped they even paid for the tent. Honestly, I'd just sleep outside. Domitian later raised it to three hundred. Still broke. Serve twenty-five years. Don't die. Then retire. One payment. Three thousand coins, or farmland. About thirteen years of starting pay. Congratulations. Now buy your own mule. Would you sign up? Yes or no?
