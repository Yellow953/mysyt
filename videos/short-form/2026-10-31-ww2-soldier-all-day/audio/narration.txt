Think WW2 soldiers spent all day storming beaches and winning battles? Yeah... not even close. Most of the time, being a soldier was basically digging holes and waiting. They dug foxholes. Fall asleep on watch? Congratulations. You're everyone's least favorite soldier. And the food? Canned meat and biscuits. Dry socks? A luxury. But when the shooting started? EVERYTHING changed. Helmets on, heads down, hoping the next shell missed. Win, and you got rest, letters, and a hot meal. Lose, and it ended VERY badly. So yeah, soldier life was mostly digging and waiting... with occasional chaos. Still wanna be a soldier?
