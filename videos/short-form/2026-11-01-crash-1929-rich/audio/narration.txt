It's September 3, 1929. Wall Street has never been higher. Everyone's rich. You know it ends in seven weeks. So you bet against the market. October 24. The floor disappears. By November, stocks are down almost half. You're rich. Cash out. Don't buy the dip yet. Stocks bounce nearly fifty percent. Everyone says it's over. It isn't. You wait almost three years. July 8, 1932. The Dow is down eighty-nine percent. Now you buy everything. It takes until 1954 to climb back. Your money grows about nine times. Predicting the crash was easy. Waiting was the hard part.
