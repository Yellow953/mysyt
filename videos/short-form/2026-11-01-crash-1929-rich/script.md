# Script: "How Rich Could You Get Knowing the 1929 CRASH?"
Batch 1/3 of style test "Modern person in the past". Source concept: Originico "rich selling burgers in ancient Rome" 4.2M and the "get rich with hindsight" Shorts (competitors.md 2b). Kept: money + hindsight + you in it. New: the hard part is not knowing the date, it is the second move (buy the bottom in 1932).

**Story spine:** Who: you with a perfect crystal ball. Want: a lot of money. Conflict: knowing isn't enough; a 50% rebound looks like the end. Turn: the money is made by waiting almost three years. Payoff: one dollar becomes about nine; the hard part was patience.
**Hook/first frame:** "It's September 3, 1929. Wall Street has never been higher." Mascot in a 1929 brokerage office holding a ticker-tape strip and grinning, overlay "SEPT 3, 1929". Co-star: no.
**Emotion:** smug confidence, tension on the bounce, relief, a dry lesson.
