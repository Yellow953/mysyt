## Fact notes
- Changed from Joe's paste: 'sergeant' -> 'instructor' (the Royal Navy has no sergeants; recruits are trained by Petty Officers/Leading Hands at HMS Raleigh, 10-week initial training).
- 'thousands of people' on a carrier: ship's company + air wing is closer to 1,000-1,600 (HMS Queen Elizabeth class); verify or say 'over a thousand'. 'aircraft worth millions' is true (F-35B is ~tens of millions each).
- Ladder is a simplified path: most officers join through the officer route, not from Petty Officer; carrier captains are rank Captain (same as level 6). Joke-level, put 'simplified ladder' in the description.
