Every level in the Royal Navy. From getting yelled at by an instructor… to commanding an entire aircraft carrier. Level one: Recruit. You can't even fold your own bed properly. Your entire personality is now shouting 'YES, SIR!' Level two: Able Seaman. You finally know how to work on a ship. Unfortunately, you're still the guy doing the jobs nobody wants. Level three: Petty Officer. Congratulations! You can now yell at recruits instead of being yelled at. Your back hurts, and your patience is gone. Level four: Lieutenant. You're an officer now. You have actual authority, important decisions, and approximately seventeen new ways to get blamed. Level five: Commander. You're running a ship or a major department. When something goes wrong, everyone suddenly knows your name. Level six: Captain. You command an entire warship. Hundreds of sailors depend on your decisions. One mistake could turn a very bad day into a national headline. Final level: Commanding a Royal Navy aircraft carrier. You're responsible for a floating city, more than a thousand people, and aircraft worth millions. Your parking space? The ocean. Would you survive level one… or quit before breakfast?
