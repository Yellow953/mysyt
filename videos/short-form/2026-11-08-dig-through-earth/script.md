# Why don't we dig a hole through the Earth? (cut-out batch 4/4, H8)
Status: PRODUCED (video + 2 covers), not uploaded. ~3.8 credits.

## Script (~52 words, est. ~27 s with Grady)
1. Why don't we just dig a hole through the Earth?
2. Fair. How hard can a hole be?
3. The deepest one we ever drilled is twelve kilometers.
4. The Earth is almost thirteen thousand kilometers wide.
5. And at twelve, it was already hot enough that we had to stop.
6. So we dug a pit. Time to fill it in.
