You wake up. The whole world's internet is gone. No email. No streaming. No cat videos. Your boss can't reach you, though. Your phone feels like a brick. It isn't. GPS still works. Those satellites don't need the internet. Saved a map earlier? It still guides you home. Nobody saved a map earlier. An old battery radio still plays the news and emergency alerts. Yes, the one in the drawer. Calls and texts? Maybe. Depends on your carrier. The bad news: almost all data between continents runs through undersea cables. The internet is basically wet. So, what would you do first?
