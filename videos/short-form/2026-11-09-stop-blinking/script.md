# What if you stopped blinking for a day? (Stage F+G, 2026-11-09)
Status: PRODUCED 2026-11-09 (script approved by Joe, used verbatim). Outline approved (hook, twist, ending A, title).

## Script (83 words, est. ~40 s with Grady; plain words, no "!")
1. Don't blink. Starting now.
2. Normally you blink about fifteen times a minute. You never notice.
3. Each blink spreads tears over your eye. It keeps it wet and wipes off dust.
4. Most people last somewhere between ten seconds and half a minute.
5. Then the sting. Then the blur. Your eyes beg you to blink, and you give in.
6. The longest unofficial record is an actor on TV: a little over an hour.
7. A full day is nearly nineteen times that.
8. You blinked. Welcome back.

## Self-review (Stage G, critic pass)
- Hook: a dare plus the premise in one picture at frame 0; the viewer is the test subject, so line 4 lands on them. Story works with no jokes: normal -> stop -> breaks -> record -> scale -> relief.
- Weakest: lines 3 and 6 are plain facts; kept short, each carried by a visual. Idea is mild, so the loop and "Welcome back" must be a real payoff.
- Originality: our own dare-the-viewer structure, own sources.

## Changes vs outline (Stage G)
- Outline said "more than twenty times that": 24 h / 1 h 17 = 18.7, so it is now "nearly nineteen times". Fixed the math.
- Merged the "ten seconds / twenty seconds" escalation into one hedged line (ten seconds to half a minute, per Sci Rep), dropped the "Normally... never notice" clutter to land at 83 words.
- Dropped "sooner or later" and "Go ahead" filler; line 3 shortened ("thin layer" cut).
- Ending A kept; "You blinked" is the first line you cannot win, and it loops.
