# Outline: What if you stopped blinking for a day? (Stage E)
Central question: how long could you really go without blinking, and what would break first?
Premise: you blink ~15 times a minute without noticing. Mr. Yellow Scarf decides to skip it for a day and his eyes veto the plan in seconds.
Mascot's stake: he wants to win a bet / not waste the "free" 5 % of his day (blinks cost time) and keep his eyes wide open like a hero. Everyman, lazy: tries the lazy shortcut of never blinking and loses.
Conflict/turn: the eye fights back. Turn = the record stare is about an hour, he needs ~24 times that.
Honesty note: the concept alone is mild; what makes it work is that the VIEWER is the test subject. So the frame-0 hook is a dare to the audience.

## Hook + frame 0 options
1. (recommended) Frame 0: whole illustrated image, mascot with huge unblinking eyes, red-rimmed, sweat drops. Line: "Don't blink. Starting now." Caption "DON'T BLINK". Punch on "now".
2. "What if you never blinked again?" over the same image.
3. "You've blinked about three times since you started watching." (curiosity, weaker claim risk)

## Beats (cut-out unless marked)
1. Hook, WHOLE IMAGE #1 (staring mascot, giant eye, clock). Earns: dare + premise in one picture.
2. "Normally you blink around fifteen times a minute. You never notice." Cut-out: mascot, tick counter climbing "15 / MIN". Earns: the normal baseline.
3. "Each blink spreads a thin layer of tears over your eye. Wipes off dust, keeps it wet." Cut-out: eyelid sweep, droplet sprite. Earns: why it matters.
4. "So you stop. Ten seconds, fine. Twenty, your eyes start to sting." Cut-out: timer 10 / 20, red veins, shaking eyelid. Earns: escalation, matches the 10-30 s range (hedged).
5. "Then the blur. The burn. Your eyes tell you to blink, and sooner or later you give in." Cut-out: blur filter on mascot, tear sprite, forced blink squash. Earns: the turn, answer to "what breaks first".
6. "The unofficial record is a TV actor, a bit over an hour. A day is more than twenty times that." Cut-out: stopwatch 1:17, then 24 H bar towering. Earns: scale; reality check.
7. Reveal/ending, WHOLE IMAGE #2 (optional) or cut-out: mascot finally blinks, relief, sparkle. Line below.

## Endings
A. (recommended) "You blinked. Welcome back." Mascot blinks big, last 0.4 s slides to frame 0 (eyes wide again, "Don't blink"). Pays off the hook, loops, replay bait.
B. "So no. Your eyes are not on a free trial." (joke, no loop).
C. "Go ahead, try it. I'll wait." (CTA-ish, weaker).

## Emotional journey
Dare (tension) -> curiosity (normal facts) -> discomfort (sting, blur) -> relief (blink) -> a smile + replay.

## Length

## Cut-out vs whole image

## Risks
- Screens-reduce-blinking claim is disputed: keep out.
- "A day is impossible" is unsourced: phrase as the answer to the premise, not a measured fact.
- Record is unofficial, via TV: say "unofficial".
- Mild idea; relies on hook and the participation mechanic.
