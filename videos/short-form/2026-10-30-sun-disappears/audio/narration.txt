You wake up and the Sun is gone. Funny thing. It's about ninety-three million miles away, so nobody notices for eight minutes. That's how long sunlight takes to reach us. So you eat breakfast under a perfectly bright sky. Gravity is just as slow. So Earth keeps circling a Sun that isn't there, like a dog chasing a ball you only pretended to throw. Then, at the exact same moment, the sky goes black and Earth lets go. It flies off in a straight line. Then it gets cold. Your coffee goes first. What would you do with your last eight minutes?
