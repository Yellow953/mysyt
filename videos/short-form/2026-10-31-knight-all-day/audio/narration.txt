Think knights spent all day jousting, rescuing princesses, and slaying dragons? Yeah... not even close. Most of the time, being a knight was mostly heavy armor and paperwork. They trained for years, getting whacked with sticks. Mess up? Congratulations. You're the stable boy. And the armor? Twenty-five kilos of steel. Bathroom breaks? Don't ask. But when war came? EVERYTHING changed. Lances down, horses charging, hoping the armor held. Win, and they got loot, land, and ransom. Lose, and it ended VERY badly. So yeah, knight life was mostly training, hunting, and paperwork... with occasional battles. Still wanna be a knight?
