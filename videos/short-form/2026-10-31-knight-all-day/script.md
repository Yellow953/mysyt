## Facts (light check; jokes exaggerate)
Knights trained from childhood (page ~7-10, squire at 14, knighted around 18; varied) and squires cared for armor and horses and practised weapons; plate armour weighed about 20-25 kg, chain mail up to ~13.5 kg; hunting was the most common leisure activity, with tournaments, falconry and games; knights often managed land and held judicial/administrative roles for their lord (hence the "desk job / paperwork" joke); captured knights were often ransomed; mounted charges with lances. Sources: https://www.worldhistory.org/Medieval_Knight/ · https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1244/the-armour-of-an-english-medieval-knight/ · https://historycrunch.com/life-of-a-medieval-knight/ (weaker, used only for the estate/administration point).
