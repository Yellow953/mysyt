## Fact notes (same limits as the long-form research.md)
- "Ten thousand bullets": 10,000 rounds supplied (verified). "Farmers paid for the bullets": Pearce's conditions (verified).
- "The gun jammed": one Lewis gun jammed at the 4 Nov dam ambush (verified; singular).
- Medals line: a politician in parliament (wording as cited; attribution kept vague).
- "The army pulled out": ~8 Nov, resumed 12-13 Nov, ended 10 Dec (so "pulled out" is true but not the whole story; the long-form covers it).
- "Lost": NOT stated as fact. Accounts differ (the army's own report claimed 986 kills). The line is framed as "by the headlines", i.e. how the press told it. Do not change to "the army lost" without hedging.
- Bounty: farmers killing emus for per-beak payments is verified; "57,034" is a single-source count with a disputed year/area, hence "one count says".
- "Under a thousand, by its own count": Meredith's report claimed 986 (disputed).
- Description note (not in narration): invented gunner; disputed figures; illustrations our own.
