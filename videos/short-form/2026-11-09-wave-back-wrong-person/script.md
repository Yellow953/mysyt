## Script (~75 words, ~38 s)
1. Someone waves at you. So you wave back.
2. Big wave. Full arm. Smile. Confidence.
3. Then you notice. They were waving at the guy behind you.
4. Now your hand is stuck in the air. Putting it down means admitting it.
5. So you commit. You fix your hair. You stretch. You become a very friendly lighthouse.   <- PEAK build
6. You walk away like this. For ten blocks.   <- ends abruptly on him still waving
