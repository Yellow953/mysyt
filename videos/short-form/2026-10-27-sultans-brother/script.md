## Facts & sources (hedges noted)
1. Murad III died 15 Jan 1595; Mehmed III left his governorship in Manisa for Istanbul and took the throne. https://www.thecollector.com/sultan-mehmed-iii-executed-nineteen-brothers/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmed_III
2. Mehmed III had nineteen brothers, executed shortly after he took the throne under the "Law of Fratricide" (the Collector: "as his very first official act"; Wikipedia: "shortly after enthronement"). Script says "Nineteen. Basically day one." (hedged "basically"; the sources differ on exact timing). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmed_III · https://www.thecollector.com/sultan-mehmed-iii-executed-nineteen-brothers/
3. Mehmed II's law (kanunname): "to whomsoever of my sons the Sultanate shall pass, it is fitting that for the order of the world he shall kill his brothers" (translations vary; wording differs by source; date not stated in script). Script paraphrase: "let a new sultan remove his brothers. Officially, for order." https://www.ekrembugraekinci.com/article/?ID=615 · https://www.dailysabah.com/columns/hakki-ocal/2020/01/07/ottomans-aint-coming
4. 1481: Bayezid (Amasya) and Cem (Karaman) were far from Istanbul when Mehmed II died; janissaries mostly backed Bayezid, who took the throne. Cem lost, found refuge in Mamluk Cairo, failed in a later Anatolia attempt, fled to Rhodes on 29 July 1482, became a captive of the Knights of Rhodes and then the Pope, and died in 1495 (Wikipedia Cem Sultan, Britannica, d'Aubusson). Script: "You flee abroad. Smart. For a while." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cem_Sultan · https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/reign-bayezid-ii-and-ottoman-civil-wars
5. Ahmed I spared his brother Mustafa in 1603; fratricide did not end for good (Osman II had his half-brother Mehmed executed in 1621), and the Kafes (named "Cage") is dated 1617-1618, Mustafa I the first inmate. Script: "Ahmed the First spared his brother. Later, sultans mostly caged brothers instead." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_I · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafes · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_I
6. "Second place gets a polite visit" and "Free rooms. No exit." are jokes (the Cage was palace house arrest under guard, not literally rent-free).
7. Not used or hedged: that fratricide was universal (not every sultan did it; the script says "a new sultan" only in the law line, and no count beyond 1595); gore or method of execution; the "Night of 19 Coffins" and the executioners' details (unverified, graphic); any claim that Cem was killed in 1481.
