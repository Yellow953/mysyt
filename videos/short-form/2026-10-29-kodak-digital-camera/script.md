## Facts & sources
1. 1975, Steven Sasson, first handheld digital camera with non-volatile storage; ~8 lb; 100x100 px (0.01 MP); cassette; 23 s per image. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Sasson . Earlier digital imaging devices existed, so "first handheld" only.
2. "A thousand times more": a typical 12-megapixel phone = 1,200x 0.01 MP (arithmetic; description says "typical phone").
3. (Round 2: the "execs doubted TV photos" line was cut for pace.) Single-source "cute, but don't tell anyone" NOT used. https://petapixel.com/2017/09/21/kodak-said-digital-photography-1975/
4. Executives reluctant to pivot; Kodak later made digital cameras (No. 1 in US digital camera sales in 2005, falling to No. 4 in 2007 per Wikipedia/IDC). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodak
5. 1979 internal report (Larry Matteson) predicted a full digital shift by 2010. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodak
6. Chapter 11 on January 19, 2012. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodak
7. "Help sink" = framing; many factors; hedge in narration and description.
