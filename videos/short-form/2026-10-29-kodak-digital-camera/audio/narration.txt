You're a Kodak engineer. It's 1975. You just built the camera that will help sink your company. Your bosses are not thrilled. The first handheld digital camera. Eight pounds. A hundred by a hundred pixels. Your phone has a thousand times more. Twenty-three seconds to save one picture. Time for a nap. Kodak sold film. Your camera needs none. Kodak's own 1979 report predicted digital by 2010. They were right. January 2012: Kodak files for bankruptcy. It did make digital cameras. Even No. 1 in the US by 2005. Film or digital, engineer? Your call.
