# Verification: Thérèse Humbert's safe (France, c.1879-1903)
Date: 2026-11-03 · Stage 1 discovery verification (not story development)

**Verdict: USABLE WITH CAVEATS.** The core story is real and the twist is solid. The caveats: loan totals are soft, the safe's contents differ by source, and nearly all detail traces back to one book (Spurling, 2000) that I could not open. Contemporary press (1902) exists but I could not read it.

## Sources actually opened
- Wikipedia, Thérèse Humbert (lead only): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Th%C3%A9r%C3%A8se_Humbert
- Evangeline Holland, Edwardian Promenade, "L'Affaire Humbert" (secondary blog; cites Spurling and B.F. Martin): https://edwardianpromenade.com/gossip/laffaire-humbert
- Hilary Spurling, review/essay on her own book, Times Higher Education, 10 Sep 1999 (Spurling is the author of *La Grande Thérèse*): https://www.timeshighereducation.com/features/playing-paris-for-a-fortune/147866.article
- Criminocorpus (French academic site on crime history), 1903 popular song records: https://complaintes.criminocorpus.org/complainte/vas-therese-son-jugement-sa-condamnation (metadata only, no lyrics)
- WebSearch result summaries only (not opened): 1902 NZ newspapers on Papers Past (e.g. https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/newspapers/AS19020512.2.49.5, redirect error), Spectator archive 24 May and 27 Dec 1902 (pages returned empty).

## Surprises and shaky points
- **Appeal depends on:** (1) the safe opening on a near-empty safe (solid, contents disputed); (2) "she sued herself with her own brothers" (partly dramatization, see #7/#8); (3) "50 million francs" (soft).
- **Safe story beats in order (all verifiable):** train meeting 1879 (her claim) -> will 1881 -> sealed safe -> nephews' lawsuit -> 20 years of loans -> Le Matin pressure -> creditors sue 1901 -> safe opened 1902 -> flight -> Madrid arrest Dec 1902 -> trial 1903.
- **Not found:** exact date of the opening; precise debt total; an independent primary-source confirmation of the safe's contents; number of ruined investors.
- The "Rente Viagère" scheme is a good extra layer (she paid old lenders with new lenders, a pyramid mechanic) but the figures are from one blog.

## Can it be told in ~5 min with surprises intact?
Yes. Hook (a crowd of 10,000 watching a safe broken open) -> backstory (train lie) -> the safe as a trick (money locked until a lawsuit ends) -> the fake family -> 20 years of loans, pyramid layer -> collapse -> reveal -> flight and arrest. All surprises hold if the wording hedges on contents and totals. Mr Yellow Scarf can play a creditor or a locksmith.
