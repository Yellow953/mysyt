# Verify R1: Nader v. Allegheny, Julian Simon, 2017 United/Dao (pillar 5)
Checked 2026-11-03. Only sources I actually opened are listed. Currency: USD nominal (1972 $32.41 and $25,000 are not inflation-adjusted).

VERDICT: USABLE WITH CAVEATS. The facts are real and strong, but three pitched claims are wrong or shaky: (a) it was a judge, not a jury; (b) Nader's punitive award did not survive; (c) the pitch's "Nader case led to / ran parallel to the auction" cannot be shown, and the Dao incident was driven by crew repositioning, not plain overbooking. Reframe and it works.

## Key beats in order (usable)
1. Airlines overbook because of no-shows; 1972 stats from the Court: 6.7 per 10,000 denied boarding, 82,000 passengers in 1972.
2. 28 Apr 1972: Nader, 107 for 100 seats, refuses $32.41.
3. Judge Richey awards $10 + $25,000 punitive (Oct 1973 per TIME); appeals; 7 Jun 1976 Supreme Court lets the claim proceed (not a payout).
4. 1968: Simon's two-page paper with the bid form, ignored; 1977-78: Kahn/CAB; 1978 volunteers-first rule.
5. 9 Apr 2017: Dao; 27 Apr settlement; United and Delta raise caps; Southwest stops overbooking.

## What is disputed or legendary
- Whether Nader's case caused the volunteer system: not shown. Honest wording: "Two separate threads. Nobody I found ties Nader's case to Simon's idea."
- Nader's money: awarded at trial, but the Supreme Court did not award it, and a case summary says it was later set aside (1980). The pitch's twist "Nader won" needs wording "won the right to sue".
- "Jury": false. Bench trial, per TIME.
- Dao: crew repositioning, not pure overbooking.
- Delta $9,950 and United $10,000: both are caps, not typical payouts.

## Enough material for about 5 minutes?
Yes, with surprises intact: a judge calling bumping "wanton", $32.41, Simon's two-page idea sitting for ten years, 2017 caps climbing to $10,000. The weak link is the ending of Nader's own case (anticlimax) and the invented causal chain. Fix by pivoting the arc: Nader is the opening hook, Simon is the hero.
