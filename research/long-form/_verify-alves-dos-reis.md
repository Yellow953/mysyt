# Verification: Alves dos Reis / Portuguese Bank Note Crisis (1925)
Date: 2026-11-03 · Stage 1 discovery verification (not story development)

**Verdict: STRONG on facts, USABLE WITH CAVEATS on the pitch.** Heavily documented (House of Lords judgment, scholarship, numismatic work). But the hunter's pitch contains several shaky or wrong specifics (see "Pitch corrections"). The story is also moderately covered on YouTube (per hunters, not re-checked).

## Pitch corrections (must change before any outline)
1. "200,000 notes / 100M escudos printed" is the first order; later orders brought it to 580,000 notes. Use "he ordered 100 million escudos' worth and then more".
2. "0.88% of GDP" is an estimate (likely one source); use "about 1%".
3. The Bank of Portugal share target: 45,000 vs ~48,500 conflicting; say "tens of thousands".
4. Sentence: 20 years vs "8 + 12 exile": hedge; he served about 15 years (1930 to 1945 is 15; jail from Dec 1925 so closer to 20 in custody; sources say "about 15" in Amusing Planet, 10 in a search summary: avoid).
5. "Ends a 150-year-old printing firm": false; Waterlow carried on until a 1961 takeover.
6. "Dutch tip": unsupported. The tip came from a Porto money-changer's employee and the newspaper *O Século*.
7. "He designed it in jail" should say "reportedly".

## Surprises and shaky points
- **Story's appeal rests on:** (1) "real notes, fake authority" (solid); (2) duplicate serial numbers exposing it (solid but note the bank discovered it by sorting notes after a teller's tip); (3) the printer made to pay (solid, £610,392); (4) link to the coup (contested).
- The "Reis planned in prison" and "Hitchcock-like" scenes are Bloom lore; fine with attribution.

## Can it be told in ~5 min with surprises intact?
Yes, but tight. Beats: the forged diploma and prison cell -> forged contract and diplomats' stamps -> Waterlow prints real money -> suitcases at the Ritz -> a private bank to launder it -> attempt to buy the Bank of Portugal -> a money-changer's suspicion and duplicate serial numbers -> arrest -> printer pays. Drop the Ambaca detail, keep coup claim to a hedged closing line. Competition: hunters report 3-4 English videos exist (unverified view counts).
