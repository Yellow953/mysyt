## Beats in order
1854 Roger lost -> 1863-65 mother advertises -> Oct 1865 Wagga Wagga butcher named -> Dec 1866 he arrives, visits Wapping -> Jan 1867 Paris, mother accepts him (income £1,000/yr) -> 12 Mar 1868 she dies -> 1871-72 civil trial lost (tattoos, French) -> 1873-74 criminal trial 188 days, 14 years -> mass popular movement, Kenealy elected 1875 -> 1884 release -> 1895 confession then retraction -> April 1898 death, coffin name.

## Twist
Intact: the courts said Orton; the public made him a hero; Lady Tichborne (the person best placed to know) accepted him; he confessed, retracted, and the coffin carried Roger's name.

## Traps / wording
- Do not say Lady Tichborne "faced the trial"; she died 12 March 1868.
- Do not claim he was proven Orton by anything but the jury and his own retracted confession.
- "Weighed X stone" and "dark room / in bed" only with attribution.
- Hunter's ~100 witnesses, "Thomas Castro answered the ad" (it was Gibbes who identified him) both need softening.
- Humour about his weight: use the Wikipedia/Woodruff numbers carefully; keep to facts and exaggeration clearly framed as joke.
