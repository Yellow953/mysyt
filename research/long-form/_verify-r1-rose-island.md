## Beats in order (safe wording)
1. 1958 idea, years of battle with sea and harbour master. 2. 20 Aug 1967 opens to tourists. 3. 1 May 1968 declares republic. 4. Late June 1968 police and boats seize it, blockade. 5. Aug 1968 demolition order; appeal rejected. 6. Feb 1969 two explosions fail to topple it. 7. 26 Feb 1969 storm ends it. 8. Rosa returns to engineering; 2009 ruins found; 2017 dies.

## Twist
Intact and honest: the navy's big explosives could not finish it, a storm did. The "only war" gag must be replaced.

## Gaps and cautions
- Strongest human detail (Rosa's personality) is thin; the Carlino carabiniere anecdote (broken Lambrusco bottle on 29 June) is a charming but memory-based, single-source scene. Label as "one carabiniere remembers".
- Source quality is Italian journalism, not archival. I did not read Rosa's own book.
- Saturation not checked (hunter says medium).
