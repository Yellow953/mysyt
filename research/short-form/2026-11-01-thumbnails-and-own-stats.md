**Observed:** women appear in the top thumbnails of 3 of 9 channels (Relic, Monk Mode, Quill). On those channels they also appear in the ordinary videos, so within a channel the woman does not separate hits from non-hits. 6 of 9 channels' biggest hits have no woman, including the biggest single hit in the survey (Day One "Discipline" 5.6M). Where a woman is in a hit, she is the premise or half of it (clothing/body taboo, a couple, lust vs love), not decoration.
**Inference:** the female character works as part of a premise about attraction, bodies or relationships, and as a channel identity; there's no sign she lifts unrelated topics. Taboo framing alone doesn't make hits either (Relic's bras 31K, bathing 4.2K, "one woman 6 men" 304).
**Test:** use the co-star only where she's part of the premise, in the first frame (our traffic is the feed, see B), and compare pairs.

## Other packaging patterns seen across the hits (observed)
- 2-3 huge words that pose the question or claim ("WHY CLOTHES?", "ONE NIGHT.", "DISCIPLINE.", "FIRST ENGINE?", "NO CLOTHES.").
- One recurring mascot with a fixed face and a brand accessory (Day One's bald guy, Monk Mode's hood, SARAAB's headband, Arnie's bald man). We already have this (the yellow scarf).
- Before/after splits that show a change in emotion or state (OminiAM dog cute → feral; "DAY 1 / YEAR 10"; "TODAY / 50 YEARS LATER").
- A face reacting to the premise, readable at phone size.
