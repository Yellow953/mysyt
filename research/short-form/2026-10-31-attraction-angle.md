Joe: the videos doing well often have a female character next to the main character (thumbnail/hook) and romantic/"lust" themes that intrigue men.

Counter-evidence (same research): clones of a hit get 0.1-1%; non-romantic ideas also hit (Omini "Every human disappeared" 1.05M, Ink Story WW2 328K, Arnie farmer 156K); our own tiny sample is too small to judge. Treat attraction as one strong lever, not a guarantee.
## The line we keep (CLAUDE.md: monetization-safe, romance cheeky never explicit)
OK: dating/courtship/marriage/flirting/attraction history, taboo-but-tasteful curiosity, a stylish confident female co-star reacting or sharing the frame, cheeky jokes.
NOT OK: nudity or revealing outfits, suggestive poses, explicit sexual descriptions, sexual violence detail (concubine/slavery topics only non-graphic), clickbait thumbnails that imply sex the video does not show (YouTube age-restricts or demonetizes these, and misleading thumbnails hurt retention).

## Frequency (Joe, 2026-10-31)
The female co-star is occasional: about 1 video in 3, mostly hook/thumbnail, never in every scene or every video. Do not generate her reference character until a chosen topic needs her.
