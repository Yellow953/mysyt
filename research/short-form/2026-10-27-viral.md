## Deconstructions (summary; full beat maps in the notes file)
1. Paint Professor, natural selection (15 min). Formula: POV-as-ancestor, one named fact per ~45 s, each tied to the viewer's own body, title payoff revealed at ~3 min.
2. Helen Christie, worst jobs (33 s). Formula: list of 5 extreme jobs at 5 s each, disgust -> status twist -> one-line punchline.
3. K12, medieval winter (2:35). Formula: romantic image vs reality, "could YOU survive", one survival need every ~15 s, a question at the end (it got no real replies).
4. Lost Legacy, Your Life as a Concubine (11 min). Formula: first-person emotional opener, "sounds fun, right? wrong" contrast, rank-up progression with a threat every ~45 s, cliffhanger per chapter.
5. OddHistory, worst jobs Ep.1 (28 s). Formula: disgust -> "but it was powerful" twist -> pinned money question.
6. 1keaa History, medieval village (55 s). Formula: dense fact list answering a search question, no character. Evergreen, low retention form.

## Gaps
- Middle East: no funny animated explainers for Baghdad / Silk Road / Mamluk life; search returns vlogs and lectures. Open lane for angle 4.
- Comment questions nobody answers well: "what would you charge for this job today?" (pinned, 5 replies); "could you survive?" (K12, 2 off-topic comments). Lesson: the question must be answerable in one word.
- Fact correction is engagement: viewers correct the whipping-boy and church-frequency facts. Facts must be right, and we can invite corrections in pinned comments.
- Fiction vs real: concubine comments say they learned the history from drama series and want the real version.
- Peasant work hours: commenters are surprised peasants worked less than people do today (1keaa). A contrast angle, not yet done on a funny channel.

## Patterns (seen in 3 or more videos)
- Titles: second person or superlative in 5 of 6 ("Your Life as...", "Could You Survive...", "POV: You're...", "The WORST jobs...", "Worst Jobs..."). Superlatives ("worst", "weirdest") in 3+.
- Hooks: 5 of 6 put the viewer in the situation or show the payoff in the first 5 to 15 s. Contrast hooks in 4 (K12 romantic -> reality, Concubine "sounds fun, right? wrong", Paint Professor, Ep.1 disgust -> power).
- Beat maps: a new item every 5 to 45 s. Every chapter ends on a payoff or a threat. Short-form uses 5 s beats; long-form uses 45 s chapters.
- Thumbnails: big bold text plus one character or reaction (Paint Professor "You can digest MILK?", Lost Legacy label cards "Worse than a Slave", "Born to Breed"). Commenters say they were tricked into clicking, which means the curiosity gap works.
- Visuals: 2D flat drawing on a plain background in 4 of 6 (Paint Professor, Lost Legacy, K12, OddHistory). Live action (Helen) works too, but it is not our format.
- CTA: 3 of 6 end with a question to commenters, and it mostly failed (K12) or worked when it was a money question (Ep.1, pinned).
