Seen live in Chrome (Shorts tab + 2 Shorts opened, frames sampled; NO audio heard, narration is German, so story beats are inferred from frames, titles, word-by-word captions).

- 52.2K subs, 85 videos. 45 Shorts on the Shorts tab, all titled "When/If <relatable social moment> 🤫 #psychology #shorts".
- Floor 16K, ~median 150K, 26/45 over 100K, two at ~1M ("crush asks you in front of everyone if you like them", "someone pushes you toward your crush"), then 774K wrong toilet, 727K friend tells your crush, 680K fight in front of girls, 676K boyfriend makes you walk awkwardly, 631K steals your fries, 514K claims you farted, 460K cheat off you, 456K phone rings in class.

- Lengths seen: 37.5 s and 44.3 s.

## Mechanism (what is the same in all 45)
1. Premise = a second-person, universal, cringe/social-pressure moment everyone has lived or feared (crush, school, public embarrassment, fart/toilet, bully). No knowledge needed to care. Title is the whole hook and uses 🤫.
2. Cast fixed: a blue 3D clay figure labelled "Du/You" + labelled others ("Crush" orange female). Labels on heads = instant read at frame 0.
3. Visual hook: glossy 3D claymation with chromatic-aberration glitch, readable in 1 s.
4. Word-by-word pop captions (one word at a time, centre screen) = fast rhythm, no dead frames.
5. Build: the "advice" voice-over escalates tension through the scene (what to do / what happens next).
6. Peak/twist: cut hard to a high-contrast out-of-world clip: a flaming red demon face (Hades-like), Daffy Duck rage, Donald Duck with lipstick marks, a calm-then-explosive anime face ("ruhig" = calm). Emotional whiplash, absurd.
7. Abrupt end on the punchline frame, no outro, so it loops/rewatches. Comments split into "relatable" vs "that's cringe/what is this" arguments.

## Risks we must NOT copy
- The peak clips are other people's copyrighted cartoons (Warner/Disney characters). That is reused-content/Content ID/copyright-strike risk and breaks our originality rule. We make our OWN peak (own art/animation).
- Der Framer's cringe/gross topics (fart, pants down) are borderline for ad suitability; we keep taste.
