## Patterns (what is NEW vs our ancient-humans angle)
1. **Timeline escalation what-if** (OminiAM): one premise, chapters "First day / week / year / 10-100 years / millions of years". Counter overlays ("1,000,000+ planes"). 1.05M on a 5K channel = the premise, not the channel. But 3 of its 10 what-ifs are flops (3-6K): only premises everyone already wonders about work (everyone disappears, last human, far future, clothes). Related-sidebar hits: "What if you had 500 IQ" 1.4M, "What if smiling got you killed" 72K.
2. **"Your life at every level of X"** (Arnie, Ranks): relatable modern jobs win (farmer, chef, casino, nightclub), niche ones flop (DoorDash 1.4K, cruise 13K). 164K subs in 18 videos = very fast growth. Our character fits (everyman, worst deal) and the format is a built-in ladder: low rung -> high rung.
3. **Day-in-the-life myth flip** (Ink Story): "What did X actually do all day?" = boring reality vs movie image. Fits our persona; matches our proven ancient-free-time angle but with a fresh subject.
4. **Arabic survival/challenge** (SARAAB): big for Arabic audience, serial (part 2, part 3 within 2 days). Needs an Arabic voice; park as a separate-channel idea for Joe (Lebanon), not Mr. Yellow Scarf's English voice.
5. **Short animated allegory** (Monk Mode, Day One): 2-3 min, blunt titles; huge but a motivational lane (different audience, mostly series). Closest fit for us: "You're Not Lazy. You're Too Comfortable." in the lazy-everyman voice. Optional later test.
6. **"How did humans invent X"** (Relic, Quill): saturated; 1 hit per ~10 videos. Avoid.

## Rules for our versions
- Copies get 0.1-1% (learnings): take the structure, change premise and persona.
- Use only premises people already wonder about (flops show niche premises die).
- Counter overlay + chapters-style escalation inside 50 s: Day 1 -> Week 1 -> Year 1 -> Year 100.
